## How to Vet a Presell Listing Before You Pay

Presells carry risk that in-stock purchases do not: you are paying now for something that ships later, often with a vague timeline. Use this checklist before you commit.

| Red flag or signal | What it usually means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| No release date stated | Seller has no confirmed allocation timeline | Ask in writing; walk away if they dodge |
| Every SKU marked sold out | Allocation exhausted, not proof of scarcity | Compare at two or three other retailers |
| Box priced above single-pack rate | Hype premium baked into the listing | Buy loose packs or wait for release |
| Vague or missing refund policy | Cancellations may be one-sided | Read the policy page before checkout |
| Bundles that force extras | Padding to raise the average order | Look for the standalone SKU |
| Third-party marketplace seller | Higher risk on fulfillment and disputes | Prefer stores with a physical address and phone |

Two habits protect you more than anything else. First, pay with a method that offers buyer protection, so a cancelled or undelivered order is recoverable. Second, keep a simple written record of the price you agreed to and the stated ship date — screenshots of the listing are enough.

Community reports from Lorcana collectors consistently describe the same pattern: presell premiums peak in the weeks right before release, then compress once boxes actually arrive and players start cracking packs on stream. Player experience also suggests that the *first* restock after launch is usually the cheapest window for anyone who missed the presell.
