## Opening Day Game Plan: Budget, Sealed, and Singles

Most players overspend during a set launch and regret it two weeks later when prices settle. A little structure fixes that. Here is a simple framework for the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City opening, scaled to how you actually play.

| Player type | Suggested spend | Best use of budget |
|---|---|---|
| Casual collector | $40–$60 | 6–8 packs plus one or two favorite singles |
| League regular | $90–$130 | Half a box, then fill deck gaps with singles |
| Competitive brewer | $180–$250 | One booster box plus targeted playsets |
| Full-set collector | $300+ | Box plus trade credit for Enchanted chases |

### Your opening day checklist

- **Set a hard budget before you leave the house** and withdraw cash if you can — it is the single most effective guardrail.
- **Bring sleeves, a playmat, and a dice set** so you can play sealed or pick-up games without borrowing.
- **Pre-sort by ink** as you open; it makes deck building at the venue dramatically faster.
- **Track your pulls in a notes app** so you know exactly what to buy as singles later.
- **Check event start times** with your local store — launch events fill up quickly.
- **Bring a trade binder** with rotated or duplicate cards; launch week is peak trading season.

Community reports consistently point to the same rookie mistake: opening every pack at the table and then having no time to actually build a deck. Open, sort, build, play. The packs will still be there afterward.

## Community Reports from the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City Opening

Player experience during launch week tends to follow a familiar rhythm, and Hyperia City is no exception. Stores running both **Core Constructed** and **Infinity Constructed** events report strong turnout, with many players bringing a sealed deck for casual games and a constructed list for the main event.

A few things the community keeps repeating:

- **Trading is hottest in the first ten days.** If you pull a Legendary you do not want, move it early — supply is tightest right after launch.
- **Do not sleep on uncommons.** Cards like Toulouse – Rough and Tumble and Pepita – Watchful Alebrije sit under fifty cents in the snapshot, and role-players are already finding homes for them.
- **Character-heavy sets reward removal.** With 186 Characters in the pool, Action cards get better than they look on paper.
- **Foil premiums fade.** Cold foil listings already outnumber nothing at launch; expect foil multipliers to compress as supply lands.

Player experience also suggests the set plays slower than the previous release. Fewer Locations and a bigger character pool means board stalls happen, so packing at least a couple of evasive or hard-removal effects is a smart hedge for your first constructed night.

If you are new to the game entirely, do not let the launch hype push you into a $250 purchase. Buy a starter product, play three or four games, and then decide. The Disney Lorcana Hyperia City opening is a great entry point precisely because the card pool is so broad — there is a viable deck for nearly every Disney fandom in here.
