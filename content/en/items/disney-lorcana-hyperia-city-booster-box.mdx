## What's Inside a Disney Lorcana Hyperia City Booster Box

A standard Disney Lorcana booster box contains 24 booster packs, and each pack holds 12 cards. That means one sealed box puts roughly 288 cards in your hands — plenty of commons and uncommons to build with, plus a stack of rares and above.

| Component | Typical Detail |

| Booster packs per box | 24 |
| Cards per pack | 12 |
| Total cards per box | ~288 |
| Foil treatment | At least one foil per pack in standard Lorcana collation |
| Rarity spread | Commons, uncommons, rares, and above |
| Best use case | Drafting, sealed play, box opening, trade stock |

Always confirm the pack count on the specific product page before you buy, since retailers occasionally list bundles, single packs, or case configurations under similar names.

At the time of writing, presale listings for the box start around $176.99 with a market price near $177.93. That puts the per-pack math in a narrow band.

Here is the reality check most buyers skip: the single most expensive non-foil card in the set, Max Goof, covers only about 9% of a box. You would need roughly eleven copies of it to break even on one Disney Lorcana Hyperia City booster box. That does not make the box a bad purchase — it makes it an entertainment and collection purchase, not an investment thesis.

Sealed prices move fast around release. A box that sits at $177 during presale can drift up if the set performs in competitive play, or soften once the market floods with opened product.

## How to Buy Without Overpaying

Use this checklist before you commit to a sealed box. It takes two minutes and routinely saves $15 or more.

| Step | What to Check |
|---|---|
| 1. Confirm the configuration | 24 packs, 12 cards each — not a bundle or single-pack listing |
| 2. Compare market price to the lowest ask | A listing far below market usually signals a condition or language issue |
| 3. Verify seller reputation | Look for high feedback volume and a clear return policy |
| 4. Check for "presale" labeling | Presale ship dates can slip; know the timeline before ordering |
| 5. Buy sealed, not "opened to verify" | Factory seals protect you on hits and misprints |
| 6. Watch the first two weeks post-release | Prices often dip once supply peaks |

For set announcements, organized play support, and official product details, check the [official Disney Lorcana site](https://www.disneylorcana.com/) before relying on third-party speculation.

## Is the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City Booster Box Worth It?

It depends entirely on why you are buying. Here is the honest split.

| Buyer Type | Verdict |
|---|---|
| Player who wants a wide card pool fast | Worth it — 288 cards covers a lot of deck-building ground |
| Draft or sealed group | Worth it — one box supports a full pod |
| Collector chasing Enchanted or Iconic pulls | Reasonable, but buy with eyes open on odds |
| Value hunter trying to profit | Skip it — buy the singles instead |
| Budget player wanting one specific Legendary | Skip the box, buy the card |

The pros are straightforward: balanced inks, a deep character pool, a strong thematic identity, and a per-pack cost that beats buying loose packs at retail markup. The cons are equally clear: top-heavy value, a thin Iconic tier, and the simple fact that most of what you open will be bulk.

If you want the thrill of a Disney Lorcana Hyperia City booster box opening, buy one box, split it with friends, and treat the pulls as a bonus. If you want specific cards for a deck, spend the same money on singles and get exactly what you need.
