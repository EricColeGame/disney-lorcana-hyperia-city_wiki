## What Is the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City Illumineer's Trove?

Hyperia City is the setting for this chapter of the Disney Lorcana trading card game, and the Illumineer's Trove is the bundle Ravensburger builds around it. It is the product most collectors gravitate toward because it is the middle ground between a small impulse purchase and a full booster box. You get enough packs to feel a real opening session without committing to a case.

### Storage and condition notes

Sealed product is only worth a premium if it stays sealed and undamaged. Keep the box out of direct sunlight, avoid stacking heavy items on top of it, and store it somewhere with stable temperature and low humidity. If you plan to resell later, do not break the factory seal to "check" the contents — that single decision typically costs you the sealed premium.

For cards you do open, sleeve anything foil or legendary immediately and keep the dividers organized by faction. The six dividers included in the Trove are genuinely useful; most players end up using all of them.
