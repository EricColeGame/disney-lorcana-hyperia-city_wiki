## Prerelease Events: The Best Value in the Preorder Window

Prerelease sealed events are where most players get their first hands-on time with a new set, and they are usually the cheapest way to open packs in a structured setting. Seating is limited, and registration typically happens separately from product reservations.

- **Register early.** Prerelease seats are capped by table space, not by product supply.
- **Bring sleeves and a playmat.** Freshly opened cards are easy to damage.
- **Expect a casual rules environment.** Judges are present, but the emphasis is on learning the new mechanics.
- **Ask about prize support.** Many stores award extra packs for participation or wins.
- **Combine errands.** If your Disney Lorcana Hyperia City preorder pickup lands the same week, collect both in one trip.

Prerelease is also the best place to learn how the set actually plays before you commit a larger budget to singles. Cards that look underwhelming in spoiler season often perform well in sealed, and the reverse is just as true.

## FAQ

**When does the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City preorder open?**
Timing depends entirely on the retailer. Some shops open reservations weeks before launch, while others wait until official product details are confirmed. Call your local store or check its preorder page regularly, since popular items can sell through within days of opening.

**Is there a purchase limit on the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City preorder?**
At many stores, yes. Community reports point to limits of one booster box and one Illumineer's Trove per customer. Limits protect supply for the wider player base, so confirm the exact policy before you pay a deposit.

**Can I get my preorder shipped?**
Often not. A significant number of independent game stores handle preorders as in-store pickup only, especially during launch week. Ask directly whether shipping is available, and get the answer in writing if delivery is a dealbreaker for you.

**Should I buy a booster box or wait for singles?**
If you plan to draft, trade, or play sealed, a booster box at preorder pricing is usually the better value. If you only want specific cards for an existing deck, waiting a few weeks for single prices to settle is almost always cheaper.

**Do I need to preregister for the prerelease event separately?**
Yes, in most cases. Product preorders and event seats are handled as separate transactions, and seating is limited by table space. Register as soon as the event is announced to avoid missing out.
