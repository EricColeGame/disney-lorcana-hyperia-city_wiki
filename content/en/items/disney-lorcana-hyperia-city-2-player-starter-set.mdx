## What's Inside the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City 2 Player Starter Set

Lorcana two-player starter sets follow a consistent formula, and the Hyperia City box is no exception. You get two complete, ready-to-play decks plus every physical component you need to actually finish a game — no extra purchases required.

| Component | Quantity | What It's For |
|---|---|---|
| Prebuilt decks | 2 decks × 60 cards (120 total) | One deck per player, legal for casual play out of the box |
| Foil cards | 2 per deck (4 total) | Showcase cards, one per deck's featured character |
| Paper damage tokens | 13 | Tracking damage on characters and locations |
| Rulebook | 1 | Learn-to-play walkthrough plus full rules reference |

A few practical notes worth flagging before you order:

- **The decks are single-product, not randomized.** You know exactly which 60 cards you're getting, which makes this a safe purchase for gift-giving.
- **Card names and rarities can shift before release.** Retail listings for presale Lorcana product explicitly warn that card details may be updated up until launch day.
- **Tokens are paper, not cardboard.** They work fine, but heavy play will wear them out — a set of acrylic or coin tokens is a cheap upgrade later.

If you already own dice or a token set from another card game, you can absolutely use those instead. The included tokens are a convenience, not a requirement.

## How the Two Hyperia City Decks Play

Each of the two prebuilt decks in the Hyperia City starter set is built around **two of Lorcana's six inks**, and each ink pairing has its own personality. That design choice matters: instead of handing you two generic beginner decks, the box gives you a taste of how deck-building actually works in this game.

Lorcana's six inks break down roughly like this:

| Ink | General Identity | What a New Player Learns |
|---|---|---|
| Amber | Support, healing, going wide with characters | How to build a board and protect it |
| Amethyst | Card draw, tricks, tricky effects | How to generate value and card advantage |
| Emerald | Evasion, tempo, disruptive characters | How to control pacing and attack angles |
| Ruby | Aggression, direct damage, speed | How to close games fast |
| Sapphire | Ramp, items, big payoffs | How to plan ahead and build toward a win |
| Steel | Removal, sturdy characters, board control | How to answer threats efficiently |

Because each Hyperia City deck pairs two inks, you get four of the six inks represented in a single purchase — a genuinely efficient way to sample the color pie before committing to a constructed deck. The playstyles are intentionally distinct, so the two decks don't feel like mirror matches when you and a friend face off.

**Community reports** from previous Lorcana starter sets suggest the decks tend to be slightly underpowered compared to tuned constructed lists, which is by design — they're teaching tools. That said, they're real decks with real synergies, and many players keep them intact as a "guest deck" for years.

## How to Get the Most Out of Your First Games

You can open the box and start playing immediately, but a little structure makes the first session much better. Here's a simple roadmap:

| Step | What to Do | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Read the rulebook's learn-to-play section once, out loud if you're teaching | 10 min |
| 2 | Play the first game with hands revealed, talking through each decision | 20 min |
| 3 | Play game two normally, no coaching | 20 min |
| 4 | Swap decks and play again — this is where the ink differences click | 20 min |
| 5 | Sleeve both decks if you plan to keep playing | 15 min |

A few extra habits that pay off:

- **Track damage carefully.** With only 13 paper tokens, games can get messy. Dice solve this instantly.
- **Don't mulligan aggressively in your first games.** Learning to play a mediocre hand is a core skill.
- **Note which ink pairing you enjoyed most.** That's your signal for where to spend money next.

## FAQ

**How many cards are in the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City 2 player starter set?**
You get 120 cards total across two prebuilt 60-card decks, including four foil cards (two per deck), plus 13 paper damage tokens and one rulebook. Nothing else is required to play a full game.

**Do I need to buy booster packs to make these decks work?**
No. Both decks are complete and playable exactly as shipped. Booster packs are for upgrading, collecting, and deck-building later — the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City 2 player starter set is designed to stand alone.

**Is this a good gift for a kid who has never played a card game?**
Generally yes. The rulebook walks through a simplified first game, the decks are prebuilt so there's no deck-building barrier, and the fixed contents mean no disappointing pulls. Ages 8+ is a reasonable expectation with some adult help on the first game.

**Should I buy now or wait for release?**
Market data from this presale window showed prices falling from roughly $69 to the low $30s as release approached. Waiting a week or two after launch usually lands you closer to a stable price — though popular starter sets can sell out at big-box retailers, so it's a trade-off between savings and availability.

**Can I combine the two decks into one 120-card deck?**
No — Lorcana decks are 60 cards, and combining them would break both the deck-size rule and the two-ink restriction. Keep them separate and use them as a matched pair for head-to-head play.
