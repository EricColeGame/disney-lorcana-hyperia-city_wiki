## Pre-Release Weekend vs. Launch Day: What's the Difference

New players often assume pre-release and launch day are the same thing. They are not, and mixing them up is the fastest way to miss the event you actually wanted.

| Factor | Pre-release (October 16) | Wide launch (October 23) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Availability | Local game stores only, limited seats | Broad retail and online availability |
| Format | Usually sealed deck from a pre-release kit | Any format, including constructed |
| Product rules | Often restricted to what is opened on site | No restrictions |
| Crowd | Smaller, more casual, community-focused | Larger, busier, more competitive |
| Best for | Trying the set early and meeting players | Buying volume and building your collection |

### Tips for Pre-Release Day

- **Pre-register the moment seats open.** Pre-release pods are capped by table space, not by product supply.
- **Bring sleeves and a playmat.** Pre-release kits do not include everything you will want for a comfortable event.
- **Build a two-ink deck with a low curve.** Sealed pools reward consistency over flashy combos.
- **Track your pulls.** Photograph your rares — it makes trading after the event much easier.
- **Ask about prize support.** Store policies vary, and knowing the payout shape changes how you build.
