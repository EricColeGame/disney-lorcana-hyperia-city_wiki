## How to Read the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City Card List

Most people skim a checklist once, feel overwhelmed, and close the tab. Read it in three deliberate passes instead, and the 242-card wall turns into a usable map.

### Pass 1: Start with collector numbers

Every card carries a number, and a complete list runs in sequence from the first card to the last. That sequence is your audit trail. Sort your binder by number, and any gap tells you exactly what is missing without cross-referencing a website. Alternate treatments — foil versions and Enchanted variants — usually sit outside the main numbered run, so track those separately.

### Pass 2: Learn the rarity symbols

Rarity is the single best predictor of what a card will cost you and how hard it will be to find in a trade binder.

| Rarity tier | How often it shows up | What it means for your plan |
|---|---|---|
| Common | Very frequently | Deck filler and draft glue; always cheap as singles |
| Uncommon | Frequently | The connective tissue of limited decks |
| Rare | Occasionally | Where most synergy payoffs live |
| Super Rare | Uncommon sighting | Often format-relevant; watch prices early |
| Legendary | Scarce | Chase cards; trade carefully and verify condition |
| Enchanted | Rarest tier, alternate art | Collector pieces, rarely deck necessities |

Ravensburger does not publish precise pull rates per tier, so ignore any post that quotes an exact percentage without a source.

### Pass 3: Read the rules text, not the character name

New players scan for familiar faces and stop there. Experienced players read abilities first and names second, because a low-rarity card with the right keyword or trigger often outperforms a flashy Legendary that does not fit any deck. When you work through the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City card list, highlight every card that interacts with your existing deck's core strategy — even the Commons.

## Turning the Checklist Into a Collecting Plan

A card list without a plan is just a shopping spree. Decide what you are actually trying to do before launch day.

| Your goal | How to use the list | Budget reality |
|---|---|---|
| Build one competitive deck | Identify 8–12 cards in your two inks | Buy singles; skip sealed entirely |
| Complete the full set | Track collector numbers, buy a box, trade aggressively | Expect several boxes plus trades |
| Chase Enchanted cards | Buy the single or trade for it | Sealed is a lottery ticket, not a strategy |
| Play limited or draft | Study the Common and Uncommon curve | Cheapest way to enjoy a new set |
| Trade and speculate | Watch Super Rare and Legendary supply | Prices shift sharply after launch weekend |

A few habits that consistently pay off:

- **Screenshot the list before launch.** Spoiler pages get edited, and early prices are driven by hype, not playability.
- **Wait two weekends before buying chase singles.** Prices on newly released Legendaries typically settle once tournament results appear.
- **Buy playable Commons in playsets early.** They are cheap, they rarely get reprinted mid-season, and they anchor budget decks.
- **Keep a want list keyed to collector numbers.** It makes trading at locals dramatically faster.
- **Check the Disney Lorcana Hyperia City card list against your existing collection** so you do not accidentally buy a fourth copy of a card you already own four of.
